  2. 12:53Version 1.0c5a3edc

    First working prototype

    Built

    • Focus button: opens on 15 min every time. Horizontal swipe picks 15 / 30 / 45 / 60, stopping at both ends. Vertical swipes do nothing, and tapping starts the session.
    • Timer: uses a fixed end time, so it stays right after backgrounding. Minutes only, rounded up. Keeps the screen awake where the browser allows it.
    • Ending: "End focus early?" confirmation. The Done screen shows a random approved quote, with the three-note chime only on natural completion.
    • Sound: White Noise, Brown Noise, Random and your own sounds. No volume slider.
    • Scenic views: six drawn scenes plus Random and your own pictures.
    • Settings: Trusted Contacts list, Alarm Safety switch with the approved wording, permissions status, and a placeholder donation sheet.
    • Honest silencing: the "Notifications silenced" line never shows in the browser. It says "Browser preview · notifications not silenced" instead.
    • Pictures, sounds and settings are stored on the device only.

    Fixed before publishing

    • Every screen was showing at once, because layout rules overrode the "hidden" attribute. Fixed with an explicit rule.

  7. 14:17Version 3.0Debug pass

    Nine bug fixes, no behavior changes

    You asked to fix anything that doesn't change what's done, and to debug.

    • A tap could be ignored: after a slide that ended off the button, the next real tap on Focus was swallowed.
    • The time picker could get stuck between two numbers if you tapped while it was still snapping.
    • A message could be invisible if it appeared while the previous one was fading out.
    • Hover effects kept running on hidden screens: the orb's ripples continued after leaving Home or the session.
    • Quick back-and-forth between screens could leave a screen half-faded or untappable.
    • Canvases were resized every frame during screen transitions. They now ignore the animation's zoom.
    • Screen readers announced "15 minutes" as soon as the page loaded.
    • Old saved choices weren't cleaned up when browser storage was unavailable. For example, a stale "Dusk Ridge" scene stayed selected.
    • Wasted work each frame: the orb's touch glow was written to the page 60 times a second even when nothing changed.

    Checked

    • Automated browser run: the picker loops both ways and ignores vertical slides. Tap-after-stray-slide works. Start → end early → Done → Home works. Rapid Settings open/close works. Back-to-back messages both show. Contacts save. 8 sounds and 8 scenes are present. No script errors.
    • The orb shader compiles and renders under WebGL.

  9. 24 Sep · 09:46V3.0 spec · browserfd2d149

    Private local search, exact spec wording

    You provided the full Focus Friend V3.0 specification: 15 chapters plus a local search system, to be built for the browser and native Android.

    • Search engine (search.js, no dependencies): normalization, tokenization, phone matching across formats, ranked tiers, bounded fuzzy matching, a tiny synonym list, did-you-mean, incremental index. No history, no network, counts-only developer logs.
    • Where search appears: Trusted Contacts as soon as one exists; sounds and scenic views only past 12 options; never on Home.
    • Keyboard and screen readers: ↓ ↑ move through results, Enter selects, Escape clears; result counts spoken after a pause in typing.
    • Exact wording: "Focus can't start without access." with "Review the rules again" / "Go back"; browser limits stated plainly for calls and alarms.
    • Contacts now store a normalized number; duplicates are caught across formats.

    Bugs the new tests caught

    • A fast flick could move two steps. It now moves exactly one.
    • A message could sit on top of a sheet button and swallow the tap.
    • The search file broke on pages that didn't declare UTF-8.

Platform limits

What the browser version can't do, and what the Android app will do instead. Nothing here is faked in the app.

Browser
Silencing notifications and calls

Web pages can't control Do Not Disturb. The Android app will, with Do Not Disturb access, and restore your settings afterward.

Browser
Trusted Contacts bypass

Only the Android app can do this. It stars your Trusted Contacts during a session and un-stars them afterward, which needs permission to edit contacts.

The APK hasn't been built or run yet: Google's servers were blocked in the build environment. Build it in Android Studio and walk through the checklist in docs/TESTING.md.

Repeat callers, alarms, emergency alerts

Built-in Do Not Disturb settings. The second call from the same person within 15 minutes rings.
