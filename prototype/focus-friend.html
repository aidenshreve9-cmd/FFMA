Focus Friend needs Do Not Disturb access

To keep you focused, Focus Friend turns on Do Not Disturb during a session:

  • Silenced: notification sounds, pop-ups, regular calls and vibration.
  • Still gets through: emergency alerts, repeat callers, your Trusted Contacts, and alarms (with Alarm Safety on).
  • Your settings go back to how they were when the session ends.
